Parameter Syntax Cheatsheet:
• w / h: Target width & height in pixels (e.g. w=800&h=450)
• fit: cover (crops to aspect ratio), contain (letterboxes), fill (stretches), inside (shrinks within bounds)
• position: Crop gravity: center, top, bottom, left, right
• cw / ch: Center crop width & height before scaling
• q: Output compression quality from 1 to 100 (defaults to 85)
• blur: Gaussian blur sigma from 1 to 100 (recommended 2-10 for placeholders)
• rotate: Lossless exact pixel rotation (90, 180, 270)
• flip=h / flop=1: Mirror horizontally or vertically
• grayscale=1 & sharpen=1: Black-and-white conversion & unsharp edge sharpening
